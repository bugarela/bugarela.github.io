Métodos Formais

These are the materials for Métodos Formais (Formal Methods), a course I taught at UDESC as a temporary lecturer. It covers temporal logic, Quint and TLA+. Everything here is in Portuguese. I keep it online for my former students, and for anyone learning formal methods. It is no longer updated.
